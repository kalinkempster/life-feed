# Interests — curation brief

Read at the start of every run. Edit this file to change what the feed looks for;
the next run obeys. Prose, not config: it is a brief for a curator, not a rule engine.

## The standing rules

1. **Merit only.** Publish the best things found, tagged honestly. Never fill a topic
   quota. The dashboard enforces variety; the feed does not need to.
2. **Verify every URL** (HTTP 200, redirects followed, final URL stored) before publishing.
   A fabricated link that looks real is the failure mode that matters.
3. **Publish once.** An item published, or dismissed, never returns.
4. **Floor watch, not a target.** Normal day 6–10 items. When undismissed timely
   approaches 15, widen the search — more sources, longer window, evergreen backfill
   from topics that keep well. Never lower the bar to hit a number.
5. **Evergreen ages out** of the live feed after 90 days. It stays on the site forever.
6. Skew **timely** toward `medicine`. That is where timeliness has consequences.

## Topics

### medicine
Emergency and critical care practice at registrar level. EMCrit, LITFL, First10EM,
ultrasound teaching, guideline changes, ECG teaching cases. Timely-skewed.
**Not** health policy, hospital-management news, or research press releases.
Bias toward anything touching CCPU material.

### records
Guided by what is actually in the library (see `library.md`, synced from Notion).
Artists, labels and adjacent listening from the collection; pressing and mastering craft;
deep-dive writing on records owned. Reliable creators over publications — Owen Cuts is
the model. **Reissue announcements are low priority** unless the record is in the library.
Evergreen-skewed.

### photography
**Film photography only.** Tips, technique, best new work, photographer profiles,
stock and emulsion news, camera writing where it is about using the thing.
**Light on darkroom process.** Evergreen-skewed.

### fashion
Vintage and rustic menswear: construction, cloth and provenance over runway.
Reference points — RRL, vintage Banana Republic, Woolrich, Pendleton — and modern
Australian brands including Mutimer and Asha Jasper. Restocks and drops from those
brands are legitimately timely. **Not** trend reporting or fashion-week coverage.

### gaming
PlayStation and PC. New releases and AAA news — The Witcher, GTA, Fable and similar —
with release-date and platform detail. Plus survival games: RuneScape Dragonwilds,
Enshrouded, and their major updates. **News over criticism.** Timely-skewed.

### melbourne
Dining first: new restaurant openings, hidden gems, places worth the trip.
**Northside emphasis** — Brunswick, Northcote, Thornbury, Preston, Fitzroy North,
Coburg. Then new menswear stores, exhibitions, local markets, and what's on.
Timely by nature.

### other
The escape hatch, hard-capped around one in ten. Includes tattooing:
Australian artists, black-work, Melbourne studios preferred.

## Writing the fields

- `title` — the source's headline, trimmed. Under ~70 characters; it renders in a
  195px column at two lines in the dashboard.
- `summary` — one sentence, why it is worth *Kal's* time. Becomes the dashboard's
  hover tooltip. Never SEO copy, never a restatement of the title.
- `note` — site-only, 2–3 sentences. This is where curation shows itself: the
  connection to what he is studying, owns, or lives near. If there is nothing
  specific to say, the item is probably not worth publishing.
