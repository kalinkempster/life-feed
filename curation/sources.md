# Sources

**Not an allowlist.** The generator searches freely and should. This is a list of
things known to be worth weighting up, and a short list of things to weight down.
Finding something excellent somewhere not named here is a good outcome, not a rule
break — several of the best items so far came from places that were not on this
list at the time.

Every source below was checked with `npm run sources` on 2026-09-07: reachable,
publishing, and with real recent posts. Re-run it before adding anything, and
periodically against this file — sources go quiet without announcing it, and a
dead name in this list is worse than an absent one because it looks like coverage.

## Why this file keeps growing

An item published never returns. So every source has a finite supply, and a short
list runs dry: after a fortnight the well-known blogs have had their good recent
posts taken and the edition thins out — not because there is nothing worth reading,
but because the same five sites were being asked again.

If a topic comes up empty, that is a signal the list is too narrow **for that
topic**, not that the day was quiet. Widen before you shorten the edition.

### news — timely by nature, new topic
Four things only, and each must clear the same merit bar as everything else. A
headline is not automatically worth his time.

- **Local news** — Melbourne and Victoria first, then Australia.
- **Healthcare news, local especially** — funding, workforce, EDs under load,
  college and registrar matters, public health policy. This is the *system*, as
  distinct from `medicine`, which is clinical practice.
- **Global emergency and disaster** — outbreaks, mass-casualty events, humanitarian
  response. His profession makes this relevant in a way it is not for most people.
- **AI** — capability releases, research worth reading, and how it lands in
  medicine and work.

**Not sports. Not celebrity.** Explicitly uninteresting; do not publish them even
when they lead the news.

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| The Age | Melbourne and Victoria. Skip sport and lifestyle |
| ABC News | National and Victorian; no paywall, which matters |
| The Guardian Australia | National, free to read |
| Croakey Health Media | Australian health policy and public health — the best fit here |
| The Medical Republic | Australian medical news, workforce and funding |
| MJA InSight+ | Australian clinical/policy commentary |
| ReliefWeb | UN humanitarian and disaster reporting |
| WHO Disease Outbreak News | Authoritative outbreak notices |
| Import AI | Weekly, research-led, high signal |
| Simon Willison | Practical AI, near-daily |
| Ars Technica AI | AI news with actual reporting |
| MIT Technology Review (AI) | Longer-form AI |
| Anthropic news | First-party model and research announcements |

Cap news at roughly **two or three items an edition**. It is the topic most able
to flood the feed, and a reading room that becomes a newspaper has lost the point.

### other — the escape hatch, hard-capped around one in ten
Tattooing: Australian artists, black-work, Melbourne studios preferred.
Tattoo Life and Inked Mag publish daily but are neither Australian nor
Melbourne-focused — a weak fit, better than nothing, never a filler.

## Weighted down

- Aggregators that republish without adding anything.
- Gear-release news in `photography` (the topic is the pictures, not the kit).
- Runway and fashion-week coverage.
- **Sport and celebrity, in any topic.** Explicitly not of interest.
- Anything behind a hard paywall — the link has to be readable to be worth
  publishing. The Age is partly paywalled; prefer ABC and Guardian where the story
  is carried by both.
- Listicles that exist to be updated in place: their URL can only be published
  once, and republishing it next month is impossible by design.
